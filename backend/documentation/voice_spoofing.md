## 1. Executive Summary & Acoustic Forensic Theory

`voice_spoofing.py` inspects audio waveforms for physical and spectral anomalies characteristic of AI voice cloning, deepfake speech synthesis, and neural vocoder reconstruction.

Physical human speech is generated by the vocal tract acting as a time-varying acoustic acoustic filter over glottal pulse excitations. Neural speech synthesizers (e.g. ElevenLabs, Tortoise-TTS, VITS) and voice converters (RVC, So-VITS-SVC) generate acoustic waveforms through deep neural vocoders (HiFi-GAN, WaveGlow, MelGAN) that map 2D mel-spectrograms into 1D pressure waves. Due to strided transpose convolutions, discrete temporal windowing, and phase estimation approximations, synthetic audio exhibits:
1. **Periodic Spectral Artifacts**: Upsampling stride overlap creates harmonic checkerboard energy in upper mel bands.
2. **Zero-Crossing Rate (ZCR) Homogenization**: Human speech transitions rapidly between turbulent unvoiced fricatives and periodic voiced vowels, producing high temporal ZCR variance. AI vocoders tend to produce unnatural stabilization across phoneme boundaries.
3. **High-Frequency Attenuation or Aliasing**: High frequencies near the Nyquist threshold ($>8\text{ kHz}$) often show uncoordinated energy spikes or artificial step cutoffs.

## 2. Forensic Preprocessing & Signal Restoration

### 2.1 Anti-Saturation De-Clipping (`declip_audio`)
When a recording microphone is overloaded by sound pressure, the audio waveform saturates at $\pm 1.0$ (clipping). In the frequency domain, hard clipping flattens peak extrema, creating artificial high-frequency square-wave harmonics that falsely trigger vocoder detection models.

```python
def declip_audio(y, threshold=0.98):
    clipped_idx = np.where(np.abs(y) >= threshold)[0]
    if len(clipped_idx) == 0:
        return y, False
    valid_idx = np.where(np.abs(y) < threshold)[0]
    if len(clipped_idx) > 0.3 * len(y) or len(valid_idx) < 2:
        return y, True 
    f = interp1d(valid_idx, y[valid_idx], kind='cubic', fill_value="extrapolate")
    y_declipped = y.copy()
    y_declipped[clipped_idx] = f(clipped_idx)
    y_declipped = y_declipped / np.max(np.abs(y_declipped))
    return y_declipped, True
```
* **Thresholding**: Identifies samples where $|y(t)| \ge 0.98$.
* **Cubic Spline Restoration**: Fits a continuous cubic spline across valid adjacent samples, smoothly bridging the clipped flat-tops.
* **Normalization**: Rescales amplitude by $\max(|y_{\text{declipped}}|)$ to prevent clipping recursion.

### 2.2 Rejection of Aggressive Spectral Gating:
The module explicitly prohibits running generic noise suppression packages (e.g. `noisereduce`):
* *Forensic Rationale*: Spectral gating artificially zeros out low-amplitude frequency bins, destroying the natural acoustic room impulse response and background thermal noise floor. This produces an artificial acoustic spectral void that mimics the exact fingerprint of an AI vocoder, causing massive false-positive convictions on authentic human speech!

## 3. Classical Acoustic Feature Extraction

Alongside deep learning inference, the engine logs three fundamental acoustic heuristics:

### 3.1 Zero-Crossing Rate (ZCR) Variance
The Zero-Crossing Rate measures the rate at which the signal changes sign:
$$\text{ZCR}(t) = \frac{1}{2(N-1)} \sum_{n=1}^{N-1} |\text{sgn}(y_t(n)) - \text{sgn}(y_t(n-1))|$$
$$\text{zcr\_variance} = \text{Var}(\text{ZCR}(t))$$
* Real human speech continuously alternates between unvoiced fricatives ($/s/$, $/f/$, high ZCR) and voiced vowels ($/a/$, $/o/$, low ZCR), generating high temporal variance.

### 3.2 Spectral Rolloff Frequency ($85\%$)
Determines the frequency $f_R$ below which $85\%$ of the total spectral distribution energy is concentrated:
$$\sum_{f=0}^{f_R} |S(f)| = 0.85 \sum_{f=0}^{f_{\text{nyq}}} |S(f)|$$
$$\text{spectral\_rolloff\_mean} = \frac{1}{T}\sum_{t=1}^T f_R(t)$$
* Synthetic text-to-speech models often exhibit an abrupt rolloff cutoff around $6000 - 7000\text{ Hz}$.

### 3.3 High-Frequency Energy Ratio ($>8000\text{ Hz}$)
Computes the proportion of spectral energy residing in the upper half of the spectrum:
$$\text{hf\_ratio} = \frac{\sum_{f > 8000\text{ Hz}} |S(f)|}{\sum_{\text{all } f} |S(f)| + 10^{-10}}$$
* Detects vocoder spectral leakage and aliasing spikes.

## 4. Mel-Spectrogram Formatting for Deep Learning

1. **Short-Time Fourier Transform & Mel Filterbank**:
   Computes $128$ mel frequency bands over a 16 kHz sampling rate:
   $$\mathbf{M} = \text{librosa.feature.melspectrogram}(y, sr=16000, n\_mels=128)$$
2. **Log-Power Scaling (dB)**:
   $$\mathbf{M}_{\text{dB}} = 10 \log_{10}\left( \frac{\mathbf{M}}{\max(\mathbf{M})} + 10^{-10} \right)$$
3. **Dynamic Range Min-Max Normalization**:
   $$\mathbf{M}_{\text{norm}} = \frac{\mathbf{M}_{\text{dB}} - \min(\mathbf{M}_{\text{dB}})}{\max(\mathbf{M}_{\text{dB}}) - \min(\mathbf{M}_{\text{dB}}) + 10^{-8}} \in [0.0, 1.0]$$
4. **Temporal Dimension Clamping (256 Time Steps)**:
   * If $T < 256$: Zero-pads right border (`mode='constant'`).
   * If $T \ge 256$: Truncates to the first $256$ frames: $\mathbf{M}_{\text{cnn}} \in \mathbb{R}^{128 \times 256}$.

## 5. Inference & Mobile Domain-Shift Veto

The normalized tensor is evaluated by `LightweightAudioAntiSpoof`:
$$\text{cnn\_score} = \mathcal{M}_{\text{voice}}(\mathbf{M}_{\text{cnn}}) \in [0.0, 1.0]$$

### 5.1 The Mobile Microphone Domain-Shift Veto
A notorious limitation of deep anti-spoofing models (including ASVspoof-trained architectures) is **domain shift**: when processing audio recorded on mobile phones or in noisy rooms, the model frequently misclassifies ambient microphone hiss and low-bitrate compression noise as vocoder artifacts, producing false alarms ($\text{cnn\_score} > 0.60$).

To prevent false convictions, the module executes an acoustic heuristic veto:
```python
if cnn_score > 0.6 and hf_ratio < 0.05 and zcr_var < 0.03:
    anomaly_score = cnn_score * 0.25
else:
    anomaly_score = cnn_score
```

* **Veto Logic**: If the CNN predicts a fake, but:
  1. The high-frequency energy is low ($\text{hf\_ratio} < 0.05$, confirming absence of vocoder metallic static), AND
  2. The ZCR variance is low ($\text{zcr\_var} < 0.03$, confirming smooth natural room acoustics),
* **Mitigation**: The CNN score is attenuated by $75\%$ ($0.25\times$), rescuing authentic mobile speech.

## 6. Visual Diagnostics Specification

The module generates `{prefix}_spec.png`, an $8 \times 6$ inch dark-themed graphic (`#0f172a` slate):

### 6.1 Panel 1 (Top): Raw Audio Waveform
* **Dimensions**: Height ratio 1.0.
* **Signal Plotted**: Time-domain amplitude $y(t)$ over duration in seconds.
* **Color**: High-contrast teal (`#14b8a6`, alpha 0.8, linewidth 0.5).
* **Diagnostic Meaning**: Exposes clipping, silence intervals, and voice activity envelopes.

### 6.2 Panel 2 (Bottom): Mel-Frequency Spectrogram
* **Dimensions**: Height ratio 2.0.
* **Display Format**: `librosa.display.specshow` mapped via `COLORMAP_MAGMA` with a vertical decibel colorbar (`%+2.f dB`).
* **Axes**: Time in seconds ($X$) vs Frequency in Hz on the logarithmic Mel scale ($Y$).
* **Diagnostic Meaning**: Human speech manifests continuous formant harmonic trajectories; AI vocoders display horizontal striations, pitch quantization blocks, and high-frequency energy voids.

## 8. Meta-Classifier Integration & Safeguards

* **Ensemble Position**: `voice_anomaly_score` feeds as **Input Feature Index 8** (`voice_score`) into the PyTorch Tabular ResNet (`ensemble_classifier.py`).
* **Interactions**:
  * Directly coupled with **Feature 12 (`sync_score`)**. When a deepfake replaces an audio track with a synthetic voice clone, both `voice_score` (detecting vocoder anomalies) and `sync_score` (detecting lip-sync desynchronization) trigger in unison.
* **Safety Guards Handled**:
  1. **Short Audio Guard**: If audio duration is $< 1.0\text{ second}$, gracefully aborts with warning `"Audio too short for spoofing analysis."` and returns neutral score `0.50`.
  2. **Missing File Guard**: Checks `os.path.exists(audio_path)`, returning an error dictionary if unreadable.
  3. **Headless Plotting**: Uses `FigureCanvasAgg` to guarantee thread-safe rendering on headless server environments.
